Log

Log a spend before you put your phone away

Type how much and what for (“uber”, “groceries”, “gym”) and Rumi files it in your category. If the category doesn't exist, it creates it. Correct one, and it learns for next time.

  • Dictate the spend with the microphone.
  • Templates for coffee, delivery, transport, salary and savings.
  • Expenses, income, debt payments and transfers between accounts.
Plan

Limits that look out for you, reminders on time

Set a monthly limit on each spend you want to watch and see how much is left before you go over.

  • A reminder the day before each loan payment.
  • Rumi spots payments that repeat, like Netflix or rent, and suggests a reminder.
  • Already paid? It won't remind you again.
Activity

Every dollar, with its day and its account

See today's, this week's or this month's transactions, and what each category cost you month by month. Tap to edit, long-press to delete.

Wealth

What you own minus what you owe

Banks, wallets like PayPal or Venmo, savings, investments, cards and loans in one place, with your net worth on top.

  • Spending only comes out of accounts with a balance.
  • Loans with their payments and what's left to pay.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rumi free?

Yes. Today Rumi is free and has no ads.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, encrypted. Rumi has no server and doesn't send your transactions over the internet. Details in the privacy policy.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You log your transactions, and Rumi never asks for bank passwords.

Does it work in US dollars?

Yes. You pick US dollars or Colombian pesos when you start, and the app follows your phone's language, English or Spanish.

What happens if I change phones?

In Settings, “Export backup” saves a file with your data that you can restore on the new phone. On iPhone, an encrypted iCloud backup also brings it over.

When is it coming to the App Store and Google Play?

Android is in closed testing on Google Play and you can join today. iPhone is coming soon.
