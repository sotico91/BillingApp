How to join

Do it from your Android phone, with the same Google account in all three steps.

  1. Join the testers group

    Sign in with your Google account and tap “Join group”. If you see “You don't have permission to access this content”, that's fine: it's about the group's messages, and the join button is still at the top.

    Open the group

  2. Accept the Google Play invitation

    Open the link and tap “Become a tester”. When it says “You're a tester”, come back to this page and go to step 3: the blue link on that page opens Google Play in the browser and may say the app doesn't exist.

    Open the invitation

  3. Install Rumi

    Download it from Google Play. If you see “Item not found”, check that the Play Store uses the same account (your photo, top right) and wait a few minutes after accepting.

    Go to Google Play
